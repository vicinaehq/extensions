# ProtonVPN

Control ProtonVPN from Vicinae on Linux.

## Commands

- **VPN Status** - View connection status, connect or disconnect, and see server details.
- **Connect to Server** - Browse countries and cities, quick connect to fastest/random server, or use special servers (P2P, Secure Core, Tor).
- **VPN Settings** - View and modify ProtonVPN settings (NetShield, Kill Switch, IPv6, etc.)

## Requirements

- [proton-vpn-cli](https://github.com/ProtonVPN/proton-vpn-cli) installed and in your PATH.
- A Proton VPN account (free or paid).
- Sign in first: `protonvpn signin`.

## Notes

- Some settings require a paid plan to change (these however are clearly marked).
